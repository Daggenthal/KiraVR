## KiraVR 1.0.0 ✨

The first release of KiraVR: PC VR streaming from SteamVR to a standalone headset, built on ALVR and focused on making Linux and SteamOS work reliably, with a setup that needs no terminal.

KiraVR keeps every setting ALVR has. It is not affiliated with the ALVR team, so please report problems [here](https://github.com/Daggenthal/KiraVR/issues).

### Highlights

- **SteamVR's official driver API on Linux:** Built on ALVR's new direct mode, with the problems from daily use fixed: No stutter when a game starts, no crash on reconnect, no wired connection stuck on "Disconnecting", and SteamVR no longer blocks the driver after a slow shutdown.
- **One AppImage:** Runs on most Linux distributions, adds itself to the app menu and carries the matching headset app.
- **Install on Headset:** Plug in the headset over USB, click once, watch the progress. adb is downloaded for you if needed.
- **System Check:** Checks Steam, SteamVR, the firewall, your GPU and hardware encoder, Vulkan layers and the network before every launch, with a Fix button for most problems.
- **In-VR performance overlay:** FPS, latency, bitrate, packet loss and the headset battery with power draw and time left. Hold both thumbsticks to show it, put it on your wrist or grab it with the laser pointer.
- **Audio follows the headset:** PC sound and microphone switch to the headset while streaming and back afterwards.
- **Diagnostics for bug reports:** Collects everything needed to look into a problem and removes your user name, PC name, addresses and serial numbers first. You read it before you share it.
- **Updates only when you want them:** KiraVR never updates by itself. A new AppImage asks twice before it replaces your version, the last 3 versions are kept, and you can switch back with a click (or `--rollback`).
- **Clear version messages:** If the headset app and the PC do not match, both sides say what to update.

### Good to Know

- **Tested on:** SteamOS on an AMD GPU (VA-API encoding) with a Quest 3. Other headsets ALVR supports should work. NVIDIA and Intel GPUs should work but are untested.
- **Linux requirements:** glibc 2.39 or newer (SteamOS, Arch, Fedora 40+, Ubuntu 24.04+), SteamVR, PipeWire.
- **Windows:** The zip is built automatically but has not been tested by anyone yet. Reports are welcome.
- **KiraVR and ALVR do not talk to each other:** Use the KiraVR headset app with the KiraVR PC app. Both can stay installed on the headset.
- **Network:** Connect the PC with a cable and use 5 GHz Wi-Fi for the headset. Wi-Fi on both ends works, but expect short hitches.
